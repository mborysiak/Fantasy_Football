## 2026-08-01 - League-Winner Upside Objective Audit

- Audited the existing objectives before changing behavior:
  - player templates use ordinary PPG, contribution, and played-game proper
    scores plus event diagnostics
  - Auction ranks paired Buy-minus-Pass mean managed-season score with an
    LCB80 uncertainty penalty
  - Snake ranks forced current-pick candidates by expected final best-ball
    score
- Froze a causal player league-winner event: active PPG at least 5.0 above the
  held-out preseason projection and managed contribution above the
  position-specific q90 of comparable preseason-ranked players from the five
  strictly prior seasons. q95 is the severity sensitivity. No target-season
  result enters a threshold.
- Replayed six matchers on 2,647 held-out targets per league. The
  `wr_ppg225_both025` arm alone improved primary q90 Brier, log loss,
  continuous tail-utility CRPS, and contribution CRPS in all four
  league-by-period core cells while staying inside the 0.25% PPG guardrail.
  The gains were small and most season-bootstrap intervals crossed zero.
- The audit exposed absolute DK tail undercalibration. Production predicted
  q90 league-winner rates of 7.45% versus 16.39% realized in 2017-2022 core
  players and 6.02% versus 14.17% in 2023-2025. Beta was much closer at
  7.72% versus 7.50% and 6.35% versus 6.94%.
- Reran the exact 384-scenario, 12-room-per-origin Phase-C banks and converted
  each room's scenario wins to championship probability. The promising WR arm
  did not transport: championship Brier/log loss worsened in DK development,
  DK recent, and beta development, improving only recent beta. Season
  intervals were wide. The prior flatter-distance finalist remained
  inconsistent as well.
- Rescored the saved nflfastR receiver and RB role experiments on +5/legacy
  impact Brier and average precision. None showed joint league/period
  replication that changes their prior rejection.
- Decision: no production matcher or Auction/Snake objective change. Add the
  rare-upside and championship scores to future validation. The next app-level
  test should preserve calibrated common scenario draws, require expected
  roster score within 0.25% of the best action, and use paired championship
  probability LCB only as a lexicographic tie-break. Causally calibrate the DK
  tail event before using absolute probabilities.
